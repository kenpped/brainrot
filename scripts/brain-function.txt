Your brain is three pounds of wet tissue and it burns twenty percent of your energy just sitting there doing nothing. That is more than your legs use walking. Every thought you have is a chemical spark jumping a gap smaller than a virus, eighty six billion neurons firing in total darkness. Here is the wild part. You do not see with your eyes. Your eyes ship garbage upstairs. Upside down, half blurry, with a hole in the middle where the nerve punches through. Your brain fills in the gap, guesses the rest, and calls it reality. It is editing this video for you right now, deleting the blur every time your eyes move. So the world you are looking at is not out there. It is a render. And it runs on chicken nuggets.
