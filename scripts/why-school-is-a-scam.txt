You spent twelve years in school and nobody taught you how taxes work. Think about that. You can name the parts of a cell. You cannot read a lease. You memorized dates for a test you forgot by summer. Nobody showed you interest rates. Nobody showed you how debt compounds. Then they hand you a diploma and a loan application on the same day. Here is the wild part. The system was built to produce factory workers. Bells. Rows. Permission to use the bathroom. That design never changed, but the world did. So school did not fail you. It worked exactly as intended. You were just never the customer. You were the product. Go learn one real skill this week. Start tonight.
